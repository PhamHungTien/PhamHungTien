# Editorial collection direction

References reviewed on 2026-10-11:
- https://paco.me/ — concise personal portfolio, direct descriptions and a clear project index.
- https://culturedcode.com/things/ — product presentation grounded in real interface imagery.
- https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/ — Liquid Glass as a floating control layer.

Our interpretation uses warm neutral surfaces, a serif accent in the home heading, real App Store artwork, and a two-column product catalogue with stable index numbers. The shared stylesheet carries the palette into product, PHTV and legal pages. The navigation retains its floating glass treatment. Hover motion is limited to a small image zoom and directional arrow response, with reduced-motion support. English remains the portfolio default and PHTV retains Vietnamese.
