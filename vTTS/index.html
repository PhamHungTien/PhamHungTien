Features

  • Listen your way

    Voices and interface in 17 languages, including English and Vietnamese. The passage being read is highlighted; tap any sentence to listen from there.

  • Books and documents

    Paste text or import EPUB, PDF, DOCX and TXT. Use on-device text recognition for scanned PDFs. Each book remembers its own position, language, voice and speed.

  • Music and sleep timer

    Change speed while listening, add background music and set a sleep timer.

  • Bookmarks, notes and stats

    Search within books, add bookmarks and notes, and track listening time, daily goals, streaks and achievements.

  • Voice Studio

    Create a reading voice from your own recording on the device. When you save, vTTS keeps the voice profile and deletes the temporary Voice Studio recording.

  • Translate and listen

    Translations run on the device and save as new books. On Apple devices, translation requires iOS/iPadOS 18 or macOS 15 or later. Android requires a language download for the translation pair first.

  • Audio and audiobook export

    Export M4A, MP3, AAC or WAV audio, or save a book as an M4B audiobook with chapter markers.

  • Backup and iCloud

    Back up your library to a file and restore it when needed. On Apple devices, you can choose to sync books, reading positions, activity, drafts, history and your pronunciation dictionary with iCloud.

  • Private and offline

    Speech is generated on your device without a cloud service. The optional LAN server is off by default and protected by an access token.

  • Free to try

    The free version has usage limits. vTTS Premium is a one-time purchase for unlimited listening, library storage and exports, plus LAN hosting. No subscription.

Questions and answers

Do I need the internet to listen to a book?

Voices come with the app. You can listen to text and imported books offline. Optional features such as iCloud sync and translation language downloads need a connection.

Can vTTS read a scanned PDF?

Choose text recognition when importing a scanned PDF. Review the recognized text before listening, especially on blurry pages or in multi-column layouts.

Is vTTS Premium a subscription?

Premium is a one-time purchase through your device’s store. It removes limits on listening, library size and exports and unlocks the local-network server.
