Features

  • Listen your way

    Voices and interface in 17 languages, including English and Vietnamese. The passage being read is highlighted; tap any sentence to listen from there.

  • Books and documents

    Paste text or import EPUB, PDF, DOCX, TXT and more. Each book remembers its own position, language, voice and speed.

  • Music and sleep timer

    Change speed while listening, add background music and set a sleep timer.

  • Bookmarks, notes and stats

    Search within books, add bookmarks and notes, and track listening time, daily goals, streaks and achievements.

  • Translate and listen

    Translate text and books on your device; the translation is saved as a new book and the original stays intact. Requires iOS/iPadOS 18 or macOS 15 or later.

  • Audio and audiobook export

    Export M4A, MP3, AAC or WAV audio, or an M4B audiobook with chapters.

  • Backup and iCloud

    Back up your library to a file and restore it when needed. Choose what syncs with iCloud: activity, books and positions, drafts, history and your pronunciation dictionary.

  • Private and offline

    Speech is generated on your device without a cloud service. The optional LAN server is off by default and protected by an access token.

  • Free to try

    The free version has usage limits. vTTS Premium is a one-time purchase for unlimited listening, library storage and exports, plus LAN hosting. No subscription.

Support

Need help or found a passage that sounds wrong? Send a description and your app version; there is no need to send an entire book or private document.
