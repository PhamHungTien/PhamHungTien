Features

  • 01

    M4B audiobooks, widgets & Siri

    Export a whole book as a chaptered M4B audiobook, add your own background music, and continue listening with widgets and Siri.

  • 02

    Vietnamese and English, on device

    Choose a voice and adjust speed while listening. Speech uses resources bundled with the app, with no additional model downloads.

  • 03

    Your own book library

    Import EPUB books, search by title or author, save favorites, and pick up where you left off. Tap a passage to start listening there.

  • 04

    Text and documents

    Paste text or import TXT, Markdown, HTML, RTF, PDF, DOCX, ODT, and FB2. Revisit reading history. Import support depends on file content; protected files may not be supported.

  • 05

    Background music and sleep timer

    Add piano music, set its volume independently, and choose when listening stops.

  • 06

    Export audio your way

    Save audio as WAV, M4A, AAC, or MP3. Include background music or export speech on its own.

  • 07

    Lock Screen playback controls

    Pause and resume with system media controls, including Dynamic Island on compatible iPhones.

  • 08

    Offline speech processing

    Generate speech without an internet connection. Books, drafts, and history are stored on your device; you choose what to export or share.

  • 09

    Optional LAN server

    A local-network speech API, off by default and protected by a token. On iPhone/iPad, keep the app in the foreground. Enable it only on a network you trust.
