This policy covers the website **phamhungtien.com**: the home page, the app pages, the Guides section and the Discussions boards. Each app (PHTV, vTTS…) has its own privacy policy, linked from the bottom of that app's page.

## Summary

- The website has no accounts of its own, no visitor analytics, and does not sell your data.
- Articles in the **Guides** section show **Google AdSense** advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve those ads. The home page and app pages carry no ads.
- The Discussions boards use your Google account through Firebase. What you post there is public.

## Data stored in your browser

- **Light or dark appearance:** your choice is kept in your browser's local storage under the key `theme`. It never leaves your device.
- **Latest download links:** the PHTV page asks GitHub for the list of releases so it can show the right download button, and caches the answer in local storage so it does not ask on every visit.

You can remove this data at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

## Google AdSense advertising

Articles in the Guides section show ads provided by **Google AdSense**:

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You may opt out of personalized advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), and opt out of other third-party vendors' cookies at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before cookies are used for personalized ads.
- Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Discussions

Each app page has a Discussions board for questions, bug reports and feedback. It runs on **Firebase** (Google):

- You sign in with your Google account. Firebase Authentication receives your display name, email address and account identifier.
- Posts and replies are stored in Cloud Firestore with your display name, account identifier and the time of posting. **Posts are public**; your email address is never shown.
- You can delete your own posts at any time. Administrators may remove content that breaks the rules.
- Firebase may keep your sign-in state in your browser. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Firebase is only loaded when you open the Discussions tab.

## Embedded content and third-party services

- **YouTube:** tutorial videos show only a preview image (served by YouTube's image servers) until you press play. The player then loads from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
- **GitHub:** the PHTV page calls GitHub's public API to find the latest download.
- **Hosting:** the website is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web server, these services may keep technical logs (IP address, browser, time of visit) to operate and protect the service.
- **Donations:** the Donate button opens PayPal or shows a bank-transfer QR code. The website never receives or stores your payment details.

These services process data under their own privacy policies.

## Children

The website is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If the way the website handles data changes, this policy will be updated and the date at the top will change with it.
