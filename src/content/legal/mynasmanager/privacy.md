My NAS Manager is a client for Synology DiskStation Manager (DSM). This policy explains how it handles server connections, files, and purchase access.

## NAS connections and data

The app sends DSM requests and file transfers to the NAS address you enter. These may carry sign-in details, files, and management requests. The developer does not operate a proxy or server for this traffic. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

The app uses your password and any one-time verification code to sign in to your NAS. It does not save those values. The saved DSM session, including its session tokens and connection details, is kept in the device's Keychain. Signing out clears the saved app session.

On a local network the system may ask for local network access to reach your NAS. The app supports HTTPS and allows plain HTTP for supported private or local addresses. A plain HTTP connection does not encrypt the traffic. If you download or share a file or create a DSM share link, copies and access at the receiving destination are governed by that destination or your NAS settings.

## App Store purchases

Apple processes purchases and restoration through the App Store. My NAS Manager uses StoreKit to check purchase access and does not receive your payment card details. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Support and policy changes

If you email us, we use the information you send to respond to your request. Please omit passwords, session tokens, and verification codes from support messages. We publish any revisions on this page with an updated date.
