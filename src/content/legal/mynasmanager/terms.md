## NAS access and management

My NAS Manager connects to Synology NAS servers running DSM. Available features depend on the server version, installed packages, and account permissions. They can include monitoring, File Station, package and service controls, and configuration of DSM's SSH service.

Only connect to systems you are authorized to access and manage. Keep your credentials and verification codes private. When you submit a command in the app, DSM applies it using the permissions of the signed-in account. Check the target and keep backups before deleting files or changing services and settings.

A working network connection to the NAS is required. Any network charges from your service provider are your responsibility.
