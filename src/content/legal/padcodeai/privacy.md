## Overview

Pad Code AI is a local-first code editor and IDE for iPad and iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

## Data We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or receive any personal data.**

- The app does not require a developer-managed account.
- It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks.
- No telemetry, crash reports, or usage data is sent to us or to anyone else.

## Your Code and Files

- Projects and source files stay on your device, in the app's storage or in folders you open from the Files app.
- Programs you run are compiled and executed on your device, inside an isolated sandbox without network access. Your code is never uploaded to run it.

## AI Assistant

The AI assistant is optional and you choose how it works in Settings:

- **On-device model (Apple Intelligence)**: prompts and code are processed on your device and are not sent anywhere.
- **A cloud provider with your own API key** (a supported third-party service or a custom compatible endpoint, depending on your region): when you send a message, your prompt is sent directly from your device to that provider. The content of the active file and a list of your project's file names are included only if you leave those options enabled in Settings. The app asks for your permission before it sends anything to a provider for the first time, and you can withdraw it at any time.

This data goes straight to the provider you selected, never to us. The provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to it.

## Credentials

API keys and GitHub access tokens you enter are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. They are only sent to the service they belong to.

## App Store Purchases

Starting with version 2.0.0, optional Premium purchases use Apple’s StoreKit. Apple processes payment and purchase restoration. The app checks verified entitlements on your device to unlock advanced tools. Your payment card details are not sent to the developer.

## Network Access

Pad Code AI uses the network for the following services:

- **Git**: cloning repositories from GitHub, and pushing commits with a personal access token you provide.
- **Cloud AI**: sending a message when you have chosen a cloud provider.
- **App Store**: loading prices, processing or restoring purchases, checking storefront availability and updating purchase rights. StoreKit may communicate with Apple outside a user-initiated purchase.
- **Links**: opening our website, Facebook page, or the App Store in your browser.

## Children's Privacy

Pad Code AI does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.
