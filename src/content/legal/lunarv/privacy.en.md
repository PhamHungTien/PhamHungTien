LunarV is a lunar calendar app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro. Lunar calendar calculations run on your device. There is no LunarV account, advertising, or third-party analytics. The developer does not run a service that receives your calendar data.

## Data on your device and in iCloud

LunarV stores app settings, date notes, family occasions, reminders, and profile details you enter, such as your date of birth, to provide calendar and personalization features. These values are stored on your device. Where iCloud is available, the app also uses your Apple iCloud account to sync these values across devices. Apple handles the iCloud data under its own privacy policy. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Calendar access and notifications

If you grant calendar access, LunarV can show upcoming events from your Apple Calendar and write the holidays and family reminders you choose to sync. The saved events can sync through the account linked to the destination calendar, such as iCloud. You can revoke calendar access in system settings and remove exported events in Calendar. If you turn on notifications and grant permission, the app schedules reminders on your device.

## App Store and external links

The App Store distributes updates and processes purchases and restoration. LunarV uses StoreKit to check Premium access. The developer does not receive your payment card details. If you open an external link, the destination's privacy practices apply. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Deletion and contact

You can edit or remove notes and occasions in the app and manage iCloud data in your Apple Account settings. If you contact us, we use the information you send to respond to your request. This page displays the date of the latest revision.
