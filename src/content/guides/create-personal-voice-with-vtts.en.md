You can use vTTS Voice Studio to hear your own notes or stories read in a voice made from your recording. The app lets you compare the result with a built-in voice by listening to the same sentence before you decide whether to save a profile. Voice Studio is available in the current Apple release. On Android, look for **Voice studio** in the version installed on your device.

## Prepare a recording you have permission to use

Use your own voice or get the speaker's explicit permission to create and use a reading voice. A file containing someone's speech does not by itself provide that permission. For your first test, choose a quiet room and read at your normal pace. A recording with one clear speaker is easier to assess than a clip with conversation, music or a television in the background.

## Record or import a sample

1. Open **Settings › Voice studio** in vTTS and choose **Create your voice**. Select a language for the sample and confirm the permission prompt.
2. Use the microphone to read the prompt shown in the app. If you already have a permitted recording, choose **Use an audio file instead** and select it.
3. Listen to the prepared sample in the review screen. In versions that show live recording feedback, a clipping, low-volume or noise warning is a good reason to try another take. Review the sound yourself even if the app reports good quality.
4. Choose **Create voice** and keep the studio open while the app processes the recording on your device.

## Audition a new sentence and use the voice

In **Try any sentence**, type a line that wasn't in the recording. For a study notebook, try “Review the first exercise, then turn to page twelve.” If you plan to listen to a whole book or a work document, also try a sentence with a name or term you expect to hear often.

Listen before selecting **Save voice**. Pick a name you can find later and use **Use this voice now** or choose it from the voice list when reading. For another language, change the reading language and listen to a short passage first. The language's speech model still controls pronunciation, so the result can sound different from your accent or pacing. If a built-in voice reads a particular passage more clearly, you can switch back in the reader. See the [vTTS reading guide](/guides/nghe-sach-epub-giong-doc-tieng-viet-voi-vtts/) for book and voice settings.

## Know what remains on the device

vTTS creates the voice and generates speech on your device. Voice Studio discards its temporary recording when voice creation finishes or you leave the flow. If you imported an audio file, the original stays wherever you selected it from. Saving a voice keeps a profile with information derived from the recording. Voice profiles are excluded from vTTS library backups and automatic sync.

You can delete the saved profile in Voice Studio. If you explicitly export a voice profile, keep track of where it goes. Someone with a compatible copy may be able to generate speech with it, and deleting your local profile leaves exported copies in their destinations. For the separate settings for books, history and iCloud, see the [vTTS privacy policy](/vTTS/privacy.html).
