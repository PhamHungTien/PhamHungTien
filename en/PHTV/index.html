Features

  • Offline and private

    Everything runs on your Mac and nothing you type leaves it. Only the GIF picker uses the Internet.

  • Fast and native

    Written 100% in Swift and tuned for macOS, with low latency in every app.

  • Smarter typing

    Spell checking, Quick Telex, automatic English restore, macros and fine controls for bilingual typing.

  • Telex, VNI and Simple Telex

    Use the method you know. Switch Vietnamese/English with Control + Shift or from the menu bar.

  • PHTV Picker and clipboard

    Insert emoji, stickers and GIFs, and bring back anything from your clipboard history.

  • The right build for your Mac

    Separate Apple Silicon and Intel builds, or install with Homebrew to pick the right one automatically.

Questions and answers

Which macOS versions does PHTV support?

macOS 14 Sonoma and later, with separate builds for Apple Silicon and Intel.

What is the easiest way to install and update PHTV?

Use Homebrew: brew install --cask phtv to install, brew upgrade --cask --greedy phtv to update and brew uninstall --cask phtv to remove. If you download manually, pick the Apple Silicon or Intel build.

How do I switch between Vietnamese and English?

Press the configured shortcut (Control + Shift by default) or choose the language from the menu bar icon.

Which typing method should I use?

Telex is the most common (ow = ơ, uw = ư, aa = â). VNI uses the digits 1–9 for marks. Simple Telex offers lighter Telex variants.

Does PHTV send my typing to the Internet?

No. PHTV works offline and collects no personal data. Only the GIF picker connects to Klipy to fetch GIFs.

Why does PHTV need Accessibility access?

To read keystrokes, switch languages and work in every app. Every macOS input method needs it.
