Features

  • Today at a glance

    Lunar date, Can Chi for day, month and year, solar term, zodiac and moon phase, with a day score, the next lucky hour and what to do or avoid.

  • Good day finder

    Find auspicious days in the next 30, 60 or 90 days for weddings, groundbreaking, openings, moving and travel, skipping Tam nương, Nguyệt kỵ and days that clash with your age.

  • Solar and lunar month

    View Gregorian and lunar dates together with holidays and notes. Convert dates using the Vietnamese calendar or look up Chinese lunar dates from 1901 to 2100.

  • Family reminders

    Save memorial days, birthdays and full-moon days by lunar or solar date, repeating yearly, monthly or weekly, with advance reminders and optional Apple Calendar sync.

  • Cultural tools

    A prayer library, first-visitor finder, yearly stars, age compatibility, Lu Ban ruler, feng shui compass and Kongming oracle, offered as cultural reference.

  • Widgets and menu bar

    Home Screen, Lock Screen and desktop widgets, the lunar date in the Mac menu bar, Siri Shortcuts, Spotlight and live world clocks.

Questions and answers

Which time zone does LunarV use for Vietnamese lunar dates?

The Vietnamese calendar uses UTC+7. LunarV also has a separate Chinese calendar mode. Check which calendar you selected, especially near the start of a lunar month or in a leap month.

Can I set a yearly reminder for a lunar memorial date?

When you create a family occasion, choose a lunar date, set it to repeat yearly and choose how far ahead to be reminded. You can also sync the occasion to Apple Calendar.

What does LunarV Premium unlock?

The calendar, date conversion, search and tools are included for free. The one-time Premium purchase removes limits on family occasions and date notes.
