7 apps

  • PHTV

    Vietnamese input method for macOS

    PHTV is an open-source Vietnamese input method for macOS 14+, with Telex, VNI, macros, spell checking, PHTV Picker and clipboard history. PHTV Keyboard is the App Store edition for Mac, iPhone and iPad.

    macOS — Open source

  • vTTS

    Books & text to speech

    vTTS reads books and text aloud on iPhone, iPad and Mac with voices built into the app and ready offline. Import EPUB, PDF and DOCX, follow the highlighted passage, add background music, translate books and export M4B audiobooks. Also on Android.

    iOS & iPadOS · macOS · Android — App Store, Google Play

  • Pad Code AI

    IDE for iPad and iPhone

    Pad Code AI runs C, C++, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Ruby, PHP, Lua and SQLite on your device with bundled compilers. It adds a free AI agent, SSH and SFTP, a project shell, LaTeX and Git.

    iOS & iPadOS — App Store

  • Pad Notes AI

    Smart notebooks

    Pad Notes AI brings handwriting, typed notes, PDF annotation and a note-focused AI assistant into one workspace. Up to 20 notebooks are free; a one-time lifetime purchase removes the limit.

    iOS & iPadOS · macOS — App Store

  • My NAS Manager

    Synology NAS manager

    My NAS Manager monitors CPU, memory, network and temperature, browses File Station, shows storage and S.M.A.R.T. status, and manages Download Station, packages and Hyper Backup. It connects only to the NAS you enter, with no accounts or tracking.

    iOS & iPadOS — App Store

  • LunarV

    Vietnamese lunar calendar

    LunarV shows lunar dates, Can Chi, solar terms and lucky hours, finds good days and remembers the occasions that matter to your family. Everything is computed on device, with no account.

    iOS & iPadOS · macOS · visionOS — App Store

  • Lunar Block

    3D block puzzle

    Lunar Block turns the falling-block puzzle into a cinematic 3D arena. Stack, spin, hold and drop crystal blocks to clear lines, and every clear shatters with real physics. No ads, no account.

    iOS & iPadOS · macOS — App Store
